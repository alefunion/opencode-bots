# Bots for OpenCode — icon notes

The mark is a square-cornered speech bubble with a stepped pixel tail and a minimal robot face (two square eyes): bots + chat, with no lettering.
Palette: near-black `#131010` (eyes), near-white `#F1ECEC` (icon field), and one flat accent, terracotta `#E2603C` (bubble).
Flat geometry only: no gradients, drop shadows, glows, or outlines around the mark.
Original artwork: it does not use or imitate the OpenCode O-frame, letterforms, or wordmark, and does not suggest Anomaly made or endorses it.
Built on a 32px grid in a 512 viewBox; the mark's 256×256 box sits inside the central 400px maskable circle and stays legible at 16px.
